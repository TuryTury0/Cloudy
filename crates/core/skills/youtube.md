# YouTube
- Search in one step: open_url https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=WORDS (spaces as +).
- Open the first result whose title matches; skip results marked Ad or Sponsored.
- The video page plays by itself; press k to pause or play, never click play twice.
- Done when the video page of the right video is open and playing.
